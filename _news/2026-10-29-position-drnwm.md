**Position: Stronger Robot Policies Do Not Always Need World Models Less** accepted to the CoRL 2026 workshop _Do Robots Need World Models?_ as a poster (top 30% of submissions). The decision value of a fixed world model depends on the policy that queries it, and can change non-monotonically as the policy gets stronger.
