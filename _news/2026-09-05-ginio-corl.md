**GINIO** accepted to **CoRL 2026** (687 of 2,094 submissions). A learned inertial measurement whose mean transforms as a vector and whose covariance transforms as a tensor under any IMU mounting — so a physically remounted IMU on a real robot drops from 8.15 m to 0.50 m ATE with no retraining.
