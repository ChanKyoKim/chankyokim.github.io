New preprint on arXiv: **E3DGS: Unified Geometric-Photometric Equivariance for 3D Gaussian Splatting via Color-as-Geometry Embedding**. We show that low-degree spherical-harmonic color is algebraically isomorphic to a rank-2 geometric tensor, enabling a unified $\mathfrak{gl}(3)$ embedding for $SE(3)$-equivariant 3DGS — without Clebsch–Gordan tensor products. [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15536)
