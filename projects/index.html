Foundation world models

Worlds that remember

A video world model forgets what leaves its context window. Give it a persistent, renderable 3D memory and it keeps seeing what it has already seen — and the scene it builds can be handed to other systems.

Toyota Research Institute · in submission (CVPR 2027)

AnyWorld

A video world model with a persistent, renderable 3D Gaussian memory — every frame lifted feed-forward into Gaussians that the generator conditions on and can export.

100% novel-view coverage at 45 m · rollouts track the recorded drive on 38/39 held-out scenes · exported 3D scene beats a 3D-conditioned baseline's own.

Post-training with RL

Trained in the loop

Imitation gives a policy the average of what it saw. Reinforcement learning in closed loop lets it improve on its own rollouts — and forces an honest accounting of how often it succeeds.

Toyota Research Institute · 2026 · interim report

Closed-loop RL for a driving policy

A diffusion-policy driving model fine-tuned with closed-loop RL in simulation, with one road-relative reward — closed-loop completion of an unseen curve tripled, seeds and limits included.

Unseen-curve completion 17% → 50% in closed-loop evaluation (1/6 → 3/6 seeds) · lane-center error 0.44–0.56 m on completed runs · three limits measured and reproduced.

Geometry for robots

Geometry that moves

Representations and learned measurements that transform lawfully as the world does — and, on top of them, dynamics as rigid-body motion on the splats, planned through in closed loop.

UMich × MBZUAI · 2026 · in progress

Gaussian World Model

A learned dynamics model over a 3D Gaussian scene — rigid-body pose updates on the splats — with a sampling planner in closed loop on top; on the Pushing Gaussians benchmark, from 0/24 to 69% per attempt.

0/24 → 69% per attempt within 30 mm · four planner-side levers, then more training · offline error did not predict closed-loop success.

CoRL 2026

GINIO

A learned inertial measurement that obeys the geometry of its sensor — the mean transforms as a vector, the covariance as a tensor — under any IMU mounting, so the estimator downstream never sees a frame it was not trained for.

Remounted IMU on a real robot 8.15 m → 0.50 m ATE with no retraining · 2.0 m vs 76.4 m under arbitrary rotation on TLIO, with 11.6× fewer FLOPs than the equivariant baseline.

arXiv 2026 · in submission (ICLR 2027)

E3DGS

What is a good latent for a 3D Gaussian? One compact gl(3) embedding carries position, covariance and color together, so a single SE(3)-equivariant network processes a splat — no Clebsch–Gordan tensor products.

Color up to SH degree 2 is a rank-2 tensor · one embedding, one backbone, one transformation law for geometry and appearance.

Reductive Lie Neurons

Equivariant neural networks for general linear symmetries on Lie algebras — twists, covariances and Gaussian shape matrices transform lawfully with the frame they are expressed in.

ReLN-Gaussian-MAE reaches 95.2% under arbitrary rotations on ShapeSplat where non-equivariant baselines fall to 18.3%.
