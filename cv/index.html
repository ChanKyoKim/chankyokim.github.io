Research Experience

  • 2026.05 – 2027.04
    Research Scientist Intern, World Foundation Model Team
    Toyota Research Institute, Los Altos, CA, USA
    • Diffusion-policy reinforcement learning (patent filed): fine-tuned a driving policy, improving full-route completion from 17% to 80% on unseen routes. Built the training and evaluation framework adopted by TRI's end-to-end driving team.
    • ActionGuide (co-author; submitted to ICLR 2027): multimodal LLM post-training to verify candidate driving paths and provide corrective guidance. Evaluated in AlpaSim with a frozen motion planner.
    • AnyWorld (lead author; under review at CVPR 2027): camera-controlled world generation with persistent metric 3D memory that directly exports as a scene asset. Achieved 8% median endpoint displacement error across six evaluation domains, vs 24–65% for compared baselines under the same sensor anchor, without alignment. Broader project training uses video, depth, and language from 3,000+ video hours plus indoor robot scans, on up to 32 GPUs.
    • VLA evaluation in generated scenes (AnyWorld × PolaRiS): developing an Isaac Sim pipeline to test policies in generated 3D scenes. Real-robot validation on TRI's DROID station is planned.
  • 2023.09 – present
    Graduate Student Researcher — Spatial AI & World Models
    University of Michigan (Prof. Maani Ghaffari), Ann Arbor, MI, USA
    • RoboBelief (joint with Prof. Xingxing Zuo, MBZUAI): fine-tuned X-VLA 0.9B with LoRA and a GRU belief filter; built a MuJoCo/LIBERO benchmark. In closed-loop simulation after 16 or 32 human actions, selected the most likely bowl location on the first choice in 160/160 trials versus 75% for a RoboMME-style memory VLA.
    • Gaussian world model and planning: modeled rigid-body motion with 3D Gaussians and integrated a sampling-based planner for closed-loop object pushing. Reached 69% success per attempt across 24 Pushing Gaussians tasks.
    • GINIO (CoRL 2026; patent filed): developed an SO(3)-equivariant neural odometry interface. After remounting a Fetch robot's IMU in unseen orientations, reduced absolute trajectory error from 8.15 m to 0.50 m without retraining.
    • Geometric learning: E3DGS (ICLR 2027 submission), Reductive Lie Neurons (ICML 2026), and ReLN-Gaussian-MAE.
