research log

Oct 20, 2026 New AnyWorld demonstrations cover metric control, additional real views, revisits, and 3D export, alongside a research note explaining the memory design.
Oct 20, 2026 ActionGuide, our work on action-grounded multimodal reasoning for closed-loop driving, has been submitted to ICLR 2027.
Sep 05, 2026 GINIO accepted to CoRL 2026 (687 of 2,094 submissions). A learned inertial measurement whose mean transforms as a vector and whose covariance transforms as a tensor under any IMU mounting — so a physically remounted IMU on a real robot drops from 8.15 m to 0.50 m ATE with no retraining. [project page]
Sep 01, 2026 AnyWorld (with TRI’s World Foundation Model team): a persistent, renderable 3D Gaussian memory keeps a video world model on the recorded drive — 38/39 held-out Waymo drives, 100% novel-view coverage at 45 m. In submission to CVPR 2027. [project page]
Aug 28, 2026 Started a collaboration with Prof. Xingxing Zuo’s group at MBZUAI on Gaussian-splatting world models for robot manipulation. First results: a learned dynamics model over the splats, planned through with MPPI, takes a 24-task push-to-goal benchmark from 0/24 to 69% per attempt. [project page]
Aug 27, 2026 AnyWorld × PolaRiS: VLA policies grasp and place inside a scene our world model generated from a single frame, and the policy ranking matches the real scan (r = 0.87) — then holds on the real robot. [project page]
Jul 17, 2026 New preprint on arXiv: E3DGS: Unified Geometric-Photometric Equivariance for 3D Gaussian Splatting via Color-as-Geometry Embedding. We show that low-degree spherical-harmonic color is algebraically isomorphic to a rank-2 geometric tensor, enabling a unified $\mathfrak{gl}(3)$ embedding for $SE(3)$-equivariant 3DGS — without Clebsch–Gordan tensor products. [arXiv]
Jun 15, 2026 Awarded the SK hynix Global Researcher Scholarship (efficient AI training & inference).
May 04, 2026 Joined the World Foundation Model team at Toyota Research Institute (Los Altos, CA) as a Research Scientist Intern — building foundation models for driving and robotics.
